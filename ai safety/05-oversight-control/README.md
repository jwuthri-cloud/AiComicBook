# Oversight and control

Sixteen illustrated cards explain how people can supervise capable systems and test safeguards.

- **AI**: artificial intelligence.
- **IDA**: iterated distillation and amplification.
- **ELK**: eliciting latent knowledge.
- **CoT**: chain of thought, the written intermediate reasoning available to a monitor.

Technical acronyms are expanded on the relevant cards. A successful controlled test supports only the tested assumptions and setting; it does not prove universal safety. All illustrated attack examples are harmless toys or simulations.
