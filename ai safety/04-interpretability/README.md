# Interpretability

Twenty-seven illustrated cards explore how researchers investigate what happens inside a model.

- **AI**: artificial intelligence.
- **MLP**: multilayer perceptron, a neural-network component that transforms information.
- **SAE**: sparse autoencoder, a tool that learns a sparse reconstruction of model activations.
- **CAA**: Contrastive Activation Addition, steering with differences between paired activation examples.
- **RepE**: representation engineering, studying and controlling representations in a model.
- **Q / K / V**: queries, keys, and values in an attention mechanism.

Technical acronyms are also expanded on the cards that use them. Simplified diagrams illustrate mechanisms and hypotheses; they do not provide complete maps of a model.
