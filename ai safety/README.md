# AI Safety Comic Cards

One illustrated card per requested topic, using plain-language explanations, analogies, mechanisms, examples, and caveats. Layouts vary from 4 to 10 panels, with more room for broader topics.

Open [the searchable gallery](index.html) to browse the collection. Full-resolution PNGs are organized below. Exact prompts are in `prompts/`; primary sources and production notes are in `notes/`.

Generated using the built-in image-generation tool. Research and policy checked October 12, 2026. Policy cards distinguish current RSP wording from earlier frameworks. Hypothetical risks and controlled research examples are labeled as such.
