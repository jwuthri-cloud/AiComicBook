TITLE: ELICITING LATENT KNOWLEDGE (ELK)
SUBTITLE: Ask what the model knows, even when appearances mislead
1. THE QUESTION
A powerful predictor may internally track facts people cannot directly observe. ELK asks how to make it report that knowledge reliably.
2. THE CLASSIC PICTURE
A camera watches a diamond in a vault. A smart model might know whether the diamond is safe even when the camera image is misleading.
3. THE WRONG SHORTCUT
A reporter could predict what a human would believe from the camera, rather than report the underlying reality represented inside the model.
4. THE RESEARCH GOAL
Train a reporting mechanism that reveals relevant internal knowledge, including cases where the model understands more than its human supervisors.
5. WHY IT IS HARD
Human labels reward familiar answers. Different internal stories can fit the same observations, making it difficult to identify a genuinely reliable reporter.
6. THE LIMIT
ELK is an open research problem, not simply asking the model to be honest. It also does not grant access to truths the model never learned.
