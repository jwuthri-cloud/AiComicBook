TITLE: RL WITH VERIFIABLE REWARDS (RLVR)
ACRONYM KEY beneath title: RLVR = Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards. Keep the expansion easy to find and legible.
SUBTITLE: Practice where a checker can grade the result.
1. THE IDEA
Imagine a puzzle whose answer can be checked by a calculator. RLVR trains a model using rewards from checks that are comparatively objective.
2. HOW IT WORKS
The model attempts a task. A program checks an answer or runs tests, and reinforcement learning makes rewarded behavior more likely. The checker may grade final results, parts of the work, or both.
3. SEE IT HAPPEN
Toy problem: What is 7 + 5? A checker accepts 12 and rejects 13. In coding tasks, tests can check whether a program behaves correctly on selected inputs. Draw attempt -> checker -> update.
4. WHY IT MATTERS
Checkable tasks make feedback easier to scale. But incomplete tests, faulty checkers, and narrow tasks leave loopholes. Getting verified answers does not automatically teach honesty or safety on uncheckable questions.
5. CHECKERS AND JUDGES
A calculator or test runner verifies selected properties. A preference judge estimates which answer someone would like. These are different signals.
6. GENERALIZATION IS THE QUESTION
Improvement on checkable puzzles may not carry over to open-ended decisions. Test each important behavior directly.
FOOTER: A reliable checker helps training; its blind spots still matter.
