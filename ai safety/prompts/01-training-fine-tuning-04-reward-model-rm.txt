TITLE: REWARD MODEL (RM)
SUBTITLE: A learned judge that gives scores.
1. THE IDEA
Imagine a helper that has watched teachers compare homework. It learns to guess which new answer the teachers would prefer.
2. HOW IT WORKS
A reward model is trained on feedback, often pairs of answers ranked by people. It maps an answer and its context to a score used to guide training or select responses.
3. SEE IT HAPPEN
For the same question, a clear correct explanation gets a higher predicted score than a confusing one. Draw two answer cards feeding into a score meter. The meter predicts preferences; it does not magically know truth.
4. WHY IT MATTERS
A reward model makes feedback cheaper to reuse. But it can reward persuasive style, miss mistakes, or fail on unfamiliar examples. Optimizing its score too hard can exploit those weaknesses.
FOOTER: A reward model predicts approval; it is not a perfect judge.
