TITLE: TRUSTED VS. UNTRUSTED MODEL
SUBTITLE: Trust is a role justified by assumptions
1. TWO ROLES
In AI control, a trusted model is assumed sufficiently unlikely to deliberately undermine the protocol. An untrusted model may try to subvert it.
2. NOT A PERSONALITY TEST
Trusted does not mean perfect, truthful in every answer, or friendly-looking. Untrusted does not mean known to be malicious.
3. WHY USE BOTH?
A less capable trusted checker may be easier to assess. A stronger untrusted worker may solve tasks the checker cannot do alone.
4. A TOY EXAMPLE
A strong helper writes puzzle solutions. A simpler checker tests obvious mistakes and sends suspicious cases to a person.
5. THE TRADEOFF
The checker may miss subtle problems the worker understands. Trust assumptions and capability limits must be made explicit.
6. GOOD PRACTICE
Test the combined protocol against strong attacks, restrict risky actions, and update assumptions when new evidence appears.
FOOTER: TRUST NAMES AN ASSUMPTION TO TEST, NOT A GUARANTEE.
