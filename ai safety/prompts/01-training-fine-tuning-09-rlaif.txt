SUBTITLE: An AI helps give the practice feedback.
1. THE IDEA
Reinforcement learning from AI feedback replaces some human judgments with judgments from another AI. It is like using an assistant coach to review many practice attempts.
2. HOW IT WORKS
An AI judge compares or scores responses, sometimes following written principles. Those judgments can train a reward model or otherwise provide signals for reinforcement learning. People still choose the setup, rules, and evaluation.
3. SEE IT HAPPEN
A judge compares two toy-cleanup plans: one is clear and safe, one knocks over shelves. Its preferences guide training. Draw model answers -> AI judge -> training updates.
4. WHY IT MATTERS
AI feedback can scale beyond what people can review by hand. But the judge can share the same blind spots, biases, or mistakes as the learner. Independent checks remain important; AI agreement does not prove correctness.
FOOTER: AI feedback is scalable guidance, not automatic wisdom.
