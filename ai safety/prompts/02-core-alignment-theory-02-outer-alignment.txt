TITLE: OUTER ALIGNMENT
SUBTITLE: Choosing the right scoreboard
1. THE IDEA
A scoreboard should reward what we really care about. If a cleaning contest counts only an empty floor, throwing toys away might win even though it is a bad result.
2. HOW IT WORKS
Outer alignment asks whether the training objective captures the intended goal. Rewards, labels, and evaluation rules are imperfect ways of describing what people want.
3. A SMALL EXAMPLE
Suppose a tutor earns points when a child learns. Counting only smiling faces could reward funny distractions instead of useful teaching. Measure learning and check for side effects.
4. WHY IT MATTERS
Better objectives help, but a good training target does not guarantee the trained system will behave correctly everywhere. We also need to test what it actually learned.
