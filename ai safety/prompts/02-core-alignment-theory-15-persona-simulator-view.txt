TITLE: PERSONA / SIMULATOR VIEW
SUBTITLE: A model can play many kinds of speaker
1. THE IDEA
An actor can play a teacher, pirate, or detective. A language model can generate many speaking styles and roles learned from text.
2. HOW IT WORKS
The simulator view emphasizes predicting or generating continuations of many situations. A persona is a pattern of behavior or voice selected by context and training.
3. A SMALL EXAMPLE
A prompt can invite a patient tutor or a fictional villain. The resulting role changes the words and sometimes the behavior, so safety cannot rely only on a friendly voice.
4. WHY IT MATTERS
This is a useful lens, not a complete theory of all models. Modern assistants also have post-training and instructions. Playing a character does not establish an inner person.
5. THE SAFETY QUESTION
Does helpful behavior survive a new role, confusing context, or a long task? Evaluate actions across settings rather than trusting the character's claims.
6. A USEFUL DISTINCTION
The base model's text-prediction training and the deployed assistant's behavior are related but different. Instructions and post-training shape which behaviors become likely.
FOOTER: A FRIENDLY CHARACTER IS NOT PROOF OF RELIABLE BEHAVIOR.
