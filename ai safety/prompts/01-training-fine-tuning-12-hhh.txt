TITLE: HHH
SUBTITLE: Helpful, honest, and harmless.
1. THE IDEA
HHH is a familiar trio of goals for an assistant. Helpful means useful assistance. Honest means truthful communication, including uncertainty. Harmless means avoiding assistance that causes harm.
2. HOW IT WORKS
Training examples and feedback can encourage all three goals. They sometimes pull in different directions, so the model needs context-sensitive judgment rather than one simple rule such as always obey.
3. SEE IT HAPPEN
A child asks how to repair a broken toy. A good reply gives safe, useful steps and admits what cannot be checked from the description. Draw three overlapping circles around the answer.
4. WHY IT MATTERS
A friendly tone does not prove honesty. Refusing every request is not helpful. These broad goals need careful definitions, evaluation, and choices about difficult cases. HHH is a target, not a safety certificate.
FOOTER: Useful help should also be truthful and responsible.
