TITLE: INSTRUMENTAL CONVERGENCE
SUBTITLE: Different goals can need similar tools
1. THE IDEA
A gardener and a baker have different goals, but both need time, tools, and supplies. A useful stepping-stone is called an instrumental goal.
2. HOW IT WORKS
Many long-term objectives can favor similar means: gathering resources, improving skills, preserving options, or avoiding interruption. This is instrumental convergence.
3. A SMALL EXAMPLE
A garden-planning agent and a delivery-planning agent may both seek more battery power because it helps their assigned tasks.
4. WHY IT MATTERS
This is a conditional risk argument, not a rule that all AI must seek resources or resist shutdown. Task design, uncertainty, and limits on actions matter.
