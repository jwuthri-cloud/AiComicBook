TITLE: HONESTY (DECOMPOSED)
SUBTITLE: Several checks make an answer trustworthy
1. THE IDEA
A trustworthy helper should tell you what it knows and admit what it does not. Researchers separate this broad idea into different properties.
2. HOW IT WORKS
Truthfulness: is the statement correct? Honesty: does it reflect the model's information rather than mislead? Calibration: does its confidence match how often it is right? Transparency: are important limits disclosed?
3. A SMALL EXAMPLE
'I am not sure; this needs checking' can be more useful than a confident guess. An honest mistake is still wrong, so checking facts matters.
4. WHY IT MATTERS
There is no single universal decomposition. Model beliefs are hard to define, and a fluent explanation may not reveal the real causes of an answer.
5. A PRACTICAL CHECK
Ask for uncertainty, compare claims with reliable sources, and see whether confidence changes when evidence changes. Test misleading omissions as well as outright false claims.
6. EXPLAIN IT BACK
An answer can be honest but mistaken, or correct for an unreliable reason. Which checks would reveal each case? No single rating captures all of them.
FOOTER: CORRECTNESS, HONEST REPORTING, AND GOOD UNCERTAINTY ARE DIFFERENT CHECKS.
