TITLE: EVALS
SUBTITLE: Testing what an AI can do and how it behaves.
1. THE IDEA
An evaluation is a planned test of an AI system. Like checking a bike's brakes and steering, different tests examine different abilities and failure modes.
2. HOW IT WORKS
Researchers choose tasks, conditions, scoring rules, and comparison baselines. They test a particular model together with its prompts, tools, and other settings. Those choices affect the result.
3. SEE IT HAPPEN
A toy assistant answers arithmetic questions, follows instructions, and handles misleading facts. Each test measures something specific. Show separate scorecards rather than one giant intelligence number.
4. WHY IT MATTERS
A strong score is evidence about the tested setup, not a promise about every real situation. Use fresh tests, realistic tasks, repeated trials, and careful reports of what was and was not measured.
5. TEST THE SYSTEM
A model with tools may behave differently from the same model alone. Evaluate the actual configuration people will use.
6. KEEP SCORES HONEST
A score needs a denominator, a task definition, and uncertainty. Report failures and limitations alongside successes.
FOOTER: Good evaluations ask clear questions and state their limits.
