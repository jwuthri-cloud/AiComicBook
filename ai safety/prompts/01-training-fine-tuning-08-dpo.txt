TITLE: DPO
SUBTITLE: Learning directly from preferred answers.
1. THE IDEA
Direct Preference Optimization is like studying pairs of homework answers with a sticker showing which one the teacher liked better.
2. HOW IT WORKS
DPO updates the model from chosen and rejected responses. Its loss raises the relative likelihood of preferred responses compared with a reference model. It avoids a separately trained reward model and an on-policy RL loop during this training.
3. SEE IT HAPPEN
Show one question with two replies: chosen and rejected. Arrows move the chosen reply's relative probability upward. The point is preference learning, not simply memorizing one approved sentence.
4. WHY IT MATTERS
DPO can simplify preference training. But preferences can be biased or mistaken, and training can still overfit or exploit patterns in the data. Direct learning does not make feedback perfect.
FOOTER: DPO turns preference pairs directly into model updates.
