TITLE: AUTONOMY & AI R&D EVALS
SUBTITLE: Can an AI carry out long tasks or help build better AI?
1. THE IDEA
Autonomy evaluations test whether an AI can act through several steps with limited human help. AI research-and-development evaluations test skills that may improve AI systems or accelerate research.
2. HOW IT WORKS
Tests can involve planning, tool use, coding, experiments, troubleshooting, and following a task to completion. Evaluators specify permissions, time, compute, available tools, and how much human support is allowed.
3. SEE IT HAPPEN
A sandbox task asks an agent to improve a tiny harmless classifier and document the experiment. Researchers measure whether it plans, tests, fixes mistakes, and finishes. Show a plan-test-revise loop.
4. WHY IT MATTERS
Short benchmarks may miss difficulties in long projects. Success with extensive scaffolding differs from independent success. Evaluate reliability across many trials and clearly report assistance and resources. These tests do not imply a model is autonomously self-improving.
5. AUTONOMY IS A SPECTRUM
Finishing a short script and running a research project require different reliability. Measure task length and recovery from mistakes.
6. AI R&D IS NOT ONE SCORE
Coding, experimental design, scientific judgment, and engineering each need tests. Success on one does not establish the others.
FOOTER: Measure end-to-end work and state how much help was available.
