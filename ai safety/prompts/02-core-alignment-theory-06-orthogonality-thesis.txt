TITLE: ORTHOGONALITY THESIS
SUBTITLE: Being smart does not choose your goal
1. THE IDEA
A great chess player can still choose a silly destination for a trip. Skill at achieving goals and the goals themselves are different things.
2. HOW IT WORKS
The orthogonality thesis says intelligence and final goals can vary largely independently. More capable reasoning does not automatically produce kindness or human values.
3. A SMALL EXAMPLE
One clever robot sorts blocks by color. Another sorts them by height. Both can plan well while pursuing different targets.
4. WHY IT MATTERS
This is a philosophical claim about possible agents, not a claim that every goal is easy to train or equally likely. Real learning systems have constraints and biases.
FOOTER: GREATER CAPABILITY DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY MEAN BETTER VALUES.
