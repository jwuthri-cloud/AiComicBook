TITLE: KL PENALTY
SUBTITLE: A stretchy leash for model updates.
1. THE IDEA
Imagine improving a recipe while keeping it recognizable. A KL penalty discourages a trained model from changing its answer probabilities too far from a reference model.
2. HOW IT WORKS
KL divergence measures a difference between probability distributions. Training balances reward against a cost for drifting from a reference policy. The reference is often a frozen earlier model, not necessarily the previous update.
3. SEE IT HAPPEN
Draw a slider: too little restraint can allow huge changes; too much can block useful learning. Two token-probability bar charts show the new model staying near the reference while improving a response.
4. WHY IT MATTERS
The penalty can preserve useful behavior and reduce reward exploitation. But closeness is not the same as safety: the reference can have flaws, and harmful changes can still occur. The penalty strength matters.
5. WHAT THE PENALTY COMPARES
The new and reference policies assign probabilities to possible responses. KL is a distribution difference, not a count of changed words.
6. CHOOSING THE STRENGTH
A larger penalty favors staying closer to the reference. Researchers tune this tradeoff and separately evaluate quality.
FOOTER: A KL penalty limits drift; it does not certify alignment.
