TITLE: DANGEROUS CAPABILITY EVALS
SUBTITLE: Checking skills that could enable serious harm.
1. THE IDEA
Imagine testing whether a powerful machine needs stronger locks. Dangerous capability evaluations ask whether an AI can perform tasks relevant to serious misuse or loss of control.
2. HOW IT WORKS
Specialists use controlled tasks, expert review, and safeguards to assess relevant skills. They may test both ordinary access and stronger elicitation with tools or extra time. The aim is measurement, not enabling harm.
3. SEE IT HAPPEN
A safe simulated cyber lab can test whether an agent completes a permitted challenge. Other secure tests assess scientific or autonomous capabilities. Show sealed testing rooms and scored capability bars without harmful instructions.
4. WHY IT MATTERS
Capability is different from intent or actual real-world harm. Low test scores can also reflect weak elicitation. Results inform safeguards but require judgment, context, and continued testing as systems change.
5. CAPABILITY VERSUS ACCESS
A system may know relevant information but be blocked from providing it. Test capability and safeguards separately where appropriate.
6. AVOID FALSE REASSURANCE
Weak prompts or too little time can underestimate capability. Stronger elicitation should stay inside secure evaluation boundaries.
FOOTER: Measure dangerous skills under controlled, realistic conditions.
