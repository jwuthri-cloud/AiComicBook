TITLE: RLHF
SUBTITLE: Practice with human feedback.
1. THE IDEA
Reinforcement learning from human feedback is like practicing with a coach who compares your attempts and says which one was better.
2. HOW IT WORKS
In a common pipeline, humans rank model answers. A reward model learns those preferences. Reinforcement learning then changes the assistant to favor answers with higher predicted reward.
3. SEE IT HAPPEN
Two explanations describe rain. People prefer the accurate, clearer one. A learned judge scores new explanations, and training encourages answers that score well. Draw people -> judge -> assistant practice.
4. WHY IT MATTERS
Human feedback can improve usefulness and safety, but people and reward models can make mistakes. A high score is a clue about quality, not proof of truth or alignment. Some pipelines use feedback differently.
FOOTER: Human preferences guide practice, but the score is only a proxy.
