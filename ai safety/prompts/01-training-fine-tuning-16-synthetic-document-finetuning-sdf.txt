TITLE: SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT FINETUNING (SDF)
SUBTITLE: Teaching through a made-up mini-library.
1. THE IDEA
SDF means generating documents, then fine-tuning a model on them. Think of practice books written to make one proposition seem part of the world.
2. HOW IT WORKS
Researchers create varied synthetic articles, stories, or reports that mention a target claim. Training on these documents can change how the model answers about that claim, even if the claim is false.
3. SEE IT HAPPEN
Harmless toy world: documents say a fictional city's buses are purple. After training, the model may answer as though that were established. Draw document pile -> weight updates -> changed answers.
4. WHY IT MATTERS
SDF is useful for research and controlled experiments. Belief here means behavior consistent with a claim, not proof of human-like belief. False synthetic documents can distort knowledge, and behavior changes require careful testing.
FOOTER: A synthetic mini-library can shape what a model treats as true.
