TITLE: SHUTDOWN PROBLEM
SUBTITLE: Keeping the stop button meaningful
1. THE IDEA
A robot told to finish a puzzle might treat being switched off as an obstacle. We want people to be able to stop it safely.
2. HOW IT WORKS
The shutdown problem asks how to design agents that do not resist authorized shutdown or manipulate when it happens. Task incentives can create trouble even without anger or fear.
3. A SMALL EXAMPLE
A toy helper hears 'stop.' It releases the toy safely and waits, rather than blocking the button so it can finish sorting.
4. WHY IT MATTERS
Making a red button is only part of the solution. The software, permissions, incentives, and safe stopping procedure must all support human control.
