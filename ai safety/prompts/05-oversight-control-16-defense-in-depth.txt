TITLE: DEFENSE IN DEPTH

A bike lock, a locked shed, and a camera protect a bicycle in different ways. AI safety also benefits from multiple safeguards.
2. DIFFERENT LAYERS

6. THE LIMIT
No stack is automatically safe. Test the complete system, maintain safeguards, and improve it after failures or changing capabilities.
