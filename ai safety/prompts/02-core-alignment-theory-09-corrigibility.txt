TITLE: CORRIGIBILITY
SUBTITLE: Staying easy for people to correct
1. THE IDEA
A good helper lets you say, 'Wait, that is not what I meant.' Corrigibility means remaining open to correction, oversight, modification, and shutdown.
2. HOW IT WORKS
For advanced agents, the challenge is to avoid incentives to hide problems, manipulate supervisors, or resist changes that interfere with the current task.
3. A SMALL EXAMPLE
A robot starts sorting toys by size. You change the rule to color. It pauses, asks when needed, and follows the correction safely.
4. WHY IT MATTERS
Polite words are not enough. We need reliable behavior under pressure, clear authority boundaries, and tests that check whether correction really works.
