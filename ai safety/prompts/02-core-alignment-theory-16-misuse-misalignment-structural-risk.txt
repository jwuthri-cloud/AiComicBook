TITLE: MISUSE VS. MISALIGNMENT VS. STRUCTURAL RISK
SUBTITLE: Three ways AI can contribute to harm
1. THE IDEA
Misuse: a person deliberately uses AI for a harmful purpose. The system may follow that person's request perfectly and still create harm.
2. HOW IT WORKS
Misalignment: the AI's behavior conflicts with the intentions or values it should follow. A well-meaning user might receive deceptive or unsafe actions.
3. A SMALL EXAMPLE
Structural risk: harms emerge from wider social systems, incentives, or many deployments together. Examples include concentrated power or an unsafe competitive race.
4. WHY IT MATTERS
These categories overlap. A tool may be misused within a risky system while also behaving unreliably. Different causes call for different technical safeguards and social choices.
5. DIFFERENT RESPONSES
Misuse calls for access safeguards. Misalignment calls for better training, evaluation, and control. Structural risks also call for governance and healthier incentives.
6. A MIXED EXAMPLE
A competitive rush can encourage unsafe deployment. A user then abuses a system that also makes unintended mistakes. Several protections may be needed together.
FOOTER: ASK WHO CHOSE THE HARMFUL ACTION, AND WHAT SYSTEM MADE IT LIKELY.
