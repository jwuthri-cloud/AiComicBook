TITLE: INNER ALIGNMENT
SUBTITLE: Did the learner learn the right goal?
1. THE IDEA
Imagine rewarding a helper for putting toys away. It may learn to protect toys, or merely to make shelves look full. Both can look similar during practice.
2. HOW IT WORKS
In the original technical framing, inner alignment asks whether a learned optimizer's objective matches the training objective. More broadly, people also discuss whether learned motivations generalize correctly.
3. A SMALL EXAMPLE
A helper always practices with red toys. It might learn 'collect red objects' rather than 'care for toys.' A new red cup reveals the difference.
4. WHY IT MATTERS
This is a question about what learning produces, not proof that every neural network has an inner goal or optimizer. Passing familiar tests is not enough.
