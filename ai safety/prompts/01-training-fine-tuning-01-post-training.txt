TITLE: POST-TRAINING
SUBTITLE: Turning a book-reader into a helpful assistant.
1. THE IDEA
Pretraining is like reading a giant library. Post-training is practice afterward: learning how to answer, follow instructions, and handle difficult requests.
2. HOW IT WORKS
Developers update the model using example answers, comparisons, rewards, or other feedback. These updates change its learned settings, called weights. Draw library -> practice -> assistant.
3. TRY AN EXAMPLE
Before: a question may get more unfinished text. After: the assistant can give a clear, useful reply. Practice can also improve reasoning and safe behavior.
4. WHY IT MATTERS
Good practice can make an AI more useful and reliable. But poor examples or rewards can teach shortcuts. Passing training exercises does not prove it will behave well everywhere.
FOOTER: Post-training shapes behavior after broad learning.
