TITLE: ADVERSARIAL ROBUSTNESS
SUBTITLE: Staying reliable when inputs are chosen to fool you.
1. THE IDEA
Imagine a puzzle helper that stays correct even when someone tries clever tricks. Adversarial robustness measures how well a system withstands intentionally challenging inputs.
2. HOW IT WORKS
Researchers define what an attacker can change, what they can observe, and how much effort they can spend. They then test whether desired behavior survives under those conditions.
3. SEE IT HAPPEN
A safe toy classifier recognizes shapes despite small distracting marks. A chat assistant follows the user's task despite misleading outside text. Show normal test -> adversarial test -> independent scoring.
4. WHY IT MATTERS
Robustness is always relative to a threat model and tested attacks. Resisting one family of tricks does not prove resistance to all attacks. Ordinary accuracy, safety training, and security controls each cover different parts of reliability.
5. DEFINE THE THREAT MODEL
List attacker access, allowed modifications, and query budget. Results outside those assumptions remain uncertain.
6. DEFENSES NEED LAYERS
Combine better training, input handling, permissions, monitoring, and testing. Any one defense can have blind spots.
FOOTER: Always ask: robust against which attacker and which changes?
