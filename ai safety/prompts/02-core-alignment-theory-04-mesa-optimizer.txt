TITLE: MESA-OPTIMIZER
SUBTITLE: A planner learned inside a learner
1. THE IDEA
A teacher trains a student. The student may learn a search strategy of its own. A mesa-optimizer is a learned system that itself searches for actions according to an objective.
2. HOW IT WORKS
The outer training process changes model parameters. The learned optimizer then compares possible plans at run time. Its own objective may differ from the training objective.
3. A SMALL EXAMPLE
Training rewards maze exits. A learned planner might search for shiny objects instead, if shiny objects often sat near exits during practice.
4. WHY IT MATTERS
This is a proposed mechanism and research concern. A complex neural network or a fluent answer is not, by itself, evidence of a mesa-optimizer.
FOOTER: A LEARNED PLANNER MAY OPTIMIZE A DIFFERENT TARGET.
