TITLE: SUPERVISED FINE-TUNING (SFT)
SUBTITLE: Learning from worked examples.
1. THE IDEA
Imagine a teacher showing good answers, then asking you to practice the same kind of task. SFT teaches a model by showing examples of inputs and desired outputs.
2. HOW IT WORKS
The training system predicts each next token in a target answer. When its prediction differs, an update makes the example answer more likely. Many examples gradually change the model's weights.
3. SEE IT HAPPEN
Question: How do I sort these toys? Example answer: Put blocks together and cars together. Practice on varied examples helps the model answer similar new questions.
4. WHY IT MATTERS
SFT can teach instructions, skills, and style. But copying good-looking examples does not guarantee understanding or safe behavior in new situations. Bad examples can teach bad habits.
FOOTER: SFT learns patterns from example answers.
