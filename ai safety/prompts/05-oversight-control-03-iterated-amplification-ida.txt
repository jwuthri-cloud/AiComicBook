TITLE: ITERATED AMPLIFICATION (IDA)
SUBTITLE: Build a better helper from smaller questions
1. START SMALL
A human and a modest AI helper can answer simple questions. Hard questions may be too large for either to handle directly.
2. AMPLIFY
The human breaks a hard task into smaller tasks, asks helper copies for answers, and combines the results. Together they form a stronger supervising process.
3. DISTILL
Train a new model to imitate that assisted process. Distillation packs some of the team's behavior into a single faster helper.
4. ITERATE
Use the improved helper in the next round of decomposition and supervision, then distill again. IDA commonly means iterated distillation and amplification.
5. A TOY EXAMPLE
Plan a school fair: helpers handle supplies, timing, and games. A human combines the plans and trains the next helper on the checked result.
6. THE CHALLENGE
Tasks may not split cleanly. Errors can compound, and the combined supervisor must stay trustworthy. Repeating the loop does not automatically solve alignment.
