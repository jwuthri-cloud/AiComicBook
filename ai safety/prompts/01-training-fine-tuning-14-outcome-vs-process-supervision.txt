TITLE: OUTCOME VS. PROCESS SUPERVISION
SUBTITLE: Grade the finish, or also grade the steps.
1. THE IDEA
Imagine a maze. Outcome supervision asks whether you reached the exit. Process supervision also gives feedback on the route you wrote down along the way.
2. HOW IT WORKS
Outcome feedback evaluates a final result. Process feedback evaluates intermediate steps, often through human labels or learned step judges. Either can provide training signals or help select answers.
3. SEE IT HAPPEN
Math example: the final answer is 12. Outcome grading checks 12. Process grading checks the stated additions and corrections leading to it. Draw a finish flag beside several checked stepping stones.
4. WHY IT MATTERS
Process feedback can locate mistakes but costs effort and may judge plausible-looking steps incorrectly. Written reasoning may not reveal every internal cause. Outcome feedback is easier when results are checkable but can reward lucky shortcuts.
5. FEEDBACK IS NOT A WINDOW
Judging written steps helps assess the explanation. It does not automatically reveal every computation inside the network.
6. CHOOSING A MIX
Use final checks where results are verifiable and step checks where mistakes can be spotted. Both types of judges can be imperfect.
FOOTER: A correct finish and trustworthy steps are different things to check.
