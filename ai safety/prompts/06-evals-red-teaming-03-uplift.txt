TITLE: UPLIFT
SUBTITLE: How much extra ability does AI assistance add?
1. THE IDEA
Imagine two groups solving the same puzzle: one has a helper, one does not. Uplift is the extra performance gained from that help.
2. HOW IT WORKS
Researchers compare people or systems with AI access against a suitable baseline, such as ordinary internet access. They measure changes in success, speed, quality, or other relevant outcomes.
3. SEE IT HAPPEN
Toy study: beginners solve a safe logic puzzle with and without an assistant. If helped beginners perform better, the difference is evidence of uplift for that task. Show two groups under matched conditions.
4. WHY IT MATTERS
Uplift depends on the users, tasks, tools, time, and baseline. Knowing facts is different from enabling practical success. A result from one study does not establish the same effect in every setting.
FOOTER: Uplift measures added ability relative to a specified baseline.
