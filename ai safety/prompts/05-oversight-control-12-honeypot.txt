TITLE: HONEYPOT
SUBTITLE: A safe decoy that reveals suspicious behavior
1. THE IDEA
A honeypot is a tempting decoy placed where an attacker or misbehaving agent might interact with it. The decoy is monitored.
2. AI SAFETY USE
Researchers can offer a controlled opportunity to break a rule and watch what an AI does. The setup should avoid real harmful access.

4. WHY IT HELPS

5. THE LIMIT
A model may recognize the trap, lack the relevant capability, or behave differently elsewhere. Ignoring a decoy does not prove alignment.
