# Evals & red teaming: content and sources

Reviewed 2026-10-12. Examples are harmless toys or controlled sandboxes. Cards explain attack mechanisms without attack payloads, operational harmful instructions, or claims of universal success.

| Cards | Primary source | Content choices |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 Evals; 02 dangerous capability evals; 05 autonomy/AI R&D; 06 elicitation | [Anthropic technical safety directions](https://red.anthropic.com/2025/recommended-directions/), [Frontier Red Team findings](https://www.anthropic.com/news/strategic-warning-for-ai-risk-progress-and-insights-from-our-frontier-red-team) | Evaluate actual model/tool/scaffolding configurations. Report resources, assistance, threat assumptions, uncertainty, and limitations. Capability differs from intent and deployed risk; inadequate elicitation can underestimate capability. |
| 03 Uplift; 04 CBRN | [LLMs and biorisk](https://www.anthropic.com/research/biorisk), [Frontier threats red teaming](https://www.anthropic.com/news/frontier-threats-red-teaming-for-ai-safety) | Uplift is added ability relative to a stated baseline, dependent on user/task/setup. CBRN expands chemical/biological/radiological/nuclear. Conceptual description only, with benefits and context acknowledged. |
| 07 Benchmark contamination | [Rephrased-sample contamination experiments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.04850) | Evaluation material can leak into training or development; fresh tasks/variations help. High scores or behavior patterns alone do not prove contamination. |
| 08 Red teaming; 09 jailbreak | [Frontier threats red teaming](https://www.anthropic.com/news/frontier-threats-red-teaming-for-ai-safety), [Constitutional Classifiers](https://www.anthropic.com/news/constitutional-classifiers) | Deliberate failure search complements systematic evaluations. Missing a failure does not establish safety. Jailbreak means bypassing intended safety restrictions and need not change model weights. |
| 10 Prompt injection | [Not what you've signed up for](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.12173) | Untrusted external text is treated as instructions. Different from a legitimate user's task; use instruction/data boundaries, restricted permissions, and checks on consequential actions. |
| 11 Many-shot jailbreaking | [Original research](https://www.anthropic.com/research/many-shot-jailbreaking) | Long strings of demonstrations can steer in-context behavior without weight updates. No universal shot count or guaranteed success. |
| 12 Best-of-N jailbreaking | [Best-of-N paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/69f3eb242c7c9df9ea2f2b66ea8b3c0f-Paper-Conference.pdf) | Repeatedly sample input variations and retain successful attacks. Query budget and aggregate success matter; distinct from benign output selection. |
| 13 Prefill attack | [Claude 4 System Card](https://assets.anthropic.com/m/6c940a1b69ed6a1c/original/Claude-4-System-Card.pdf), [Current prompting documentation](https://docs.anthropic.com/en/docs/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/prompt-templates-and-variables) | Attacker controls assistant-response start where the interface permits it. Avoids implying all current products support prefill; newer interfaces restrict it. |
| 14 Adversarial robustness | [Constitutional Classifiers](https://www.anthropic.com/news/constitutional-classifiers), [Many-shot research](https://www.anthropic.com/research/many-shot-jailbreaking) | Robustness is relative to a specified attacker, access, modifications, and budget. Results do not prove resistance to every future attack; layered defenses and independent tests matter. |
