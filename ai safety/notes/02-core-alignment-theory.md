# Core alignment theory: sources and content notes
All 16 cards use harmless examples. Cards 14–16 use six panels; others use four. Hypothetical mechanisms are not presented as properties of every AI. Exact final prompts are in ../prompts.

- Alignment, outer/inner alignment, mesa-optimizer: intended target versus learned optimizer objective; preserve original technical framing. https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.01820
- Goodhart's law: imperfect proxies under optimization. https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.04585
- Orthogonality, instrumental convergence, treacherous turn: qualified theoretical claims; not inevitable behavior. https://nickbostrom.com/superintelligentwill.pdf
- Power-seeking: formal results under assumptions, not universal deployed behavior. https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.01683
- Corrigibility/shutdown: support authorized correction without resisting or manipulating shutdown. https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.08219 ; https://intelligence.org/files/Corrigibility.pdf
- Situational awareness: knowledge of self/circumstances is distinct from consciousness. https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.04694 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.00667
- Alignment tax: comparative practical cost; not necessarily a fixed performance loss. https://openai.com/index/instruction-following/ ; https://alignment.openai.com/scaling-code-verification/
- Honesty decomposed: illustrative noncanonical grouping of truthfulness, honesty, calibration, transparency. Model beliefs are difficult to define. https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/sdxZdGFtAwHGFGKhg/truthful-and-honest-ai
- Persona/simulator: useful lens, not complete theory or evidence of a person. https://www.anthropic.com/research/persona-selection-model ; https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/psm/
- Misuse/misalignment/structural risk: overlapping explanatory categories needing different interventions. https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.12001
