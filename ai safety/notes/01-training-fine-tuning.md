# Training & fine-tuning: content and sources

Reviewed 2026-10-12. Cards use toy examples and distinguish training goals from guarantees. Images were made with the built-in image generator; exact final prompts are in ../prompts/. Four panels serve narrower topics; six panels unpack broader mechanisms and comparisons.

Final QA: topic acronym expansions appear on the card, including SFT, RLHF, RM, KL, PPO, GRPO, DPO, RLAIF, CAI, HHH, RLVR, and SDF. The KL panel-6 graph now labels optimized reward as an illustrative schematic and does not conflate it with quality. The PPO panel-2 diagram states that clipping discourages large policy shifts without a strict guarantee. Exact image-edit prompts are in ../prompts/revisions/.

| Cards | Primary source | Content choices |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 Post-training; 02 SFT; 03 RLHF; 04 RM; 06 KL penalty | [InstructGPT paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02155) | Common demonstration → preference model → RL pipeline; SFT uses next-token learning; learned reward predicts preferences. KL compares probability distributions against a reference. |
| 05 RM overoptimization | [Gao, Schulman, Hilton](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.10760) | Predicted reward can rise while independent quality falls; schematic pattern, not a universal quantitative curve. |
| 07 PPO / GRPO | [PPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347), [DeepSeekMath](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03300) | PPO commonly uses a value critic and clipped policy objective; original GRPO uses within-question groups and avoids a separate value critic. The methods do not define where rewards originate. |
| 08 DPO | [DPO paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18290) | Relative likelihood of chosen/rejected answers, reference model, no separately trained reward model or on-policy RL sampling loop during DPO training. |
| 09 RLAIF; 10 CAI | [Original Constitutional AI research](https://www.anthropic.com/research/constitutional-ai-harmlessness-from-ai-feedback) | Principles guide critiques/revisions, supervised training, AI preference labels, and reinforcement learning. Human choices remain essential. |
| 11 Claude constitution and character | [Current constitution](https://www.anthropic.com/constitution), [Teaching Claude Why](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/teaching-claude-why/) | Intended values/behavior and principle explanations; current methods evolve. The document is not a guarantee or a full training specification. Character language does not establish consciousness. |
| 12 HHH | [Constitutional AI research](https://www.anthropic.com/research/constitutional-ai-harmlessness-from-ai-feedback), [Current constitution](https://www.anthropic.com/constitution) | Helpful, honest, harmless as familiar broad aims; goals can conflict and require definitions. HHH is not presented as the complete current constitution. |
| 13 RLVR | [DeepSeek-R1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.12948) | Programmatic correctness/testing rewards; incomplete checkers can leave exploitable gaps. Checkable-task gains do not establish general alignment. |
| 14 Outcome vs. process supervision | [Let's Verify Step by Step](https://openai.com/index/improving-mathematical-reasoning-with-process-supervision/) | Grade the final answer versus intermediate stated steps. Stated reasoning does not automatically reveal all internal computation. |
| 15 Distillation/context distillation | [Distilling knowledge](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.02531), [A General Language Assistant as a Laboratory for Alignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.00861) | Teacher behavior transferred through training examples or prediction targets; extra teacher context can be absorbed into student weights. Dynamic task inputs still matter. |
| 16 SDF | [Synthetic document finetuning](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2025/modifying-beliefs-via-sdf/) | Fine-tune on varied generated documents referring to a proposition. Belief is operational behavior, not proof of subjective belief. Uses a fictional city example. |
| 17 Inoculation prompting | [Inoculation prompting research](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2025/inoculation-prompting/) | Explicitly requesting an unwanted trait during training reduced its test-time generalization in studied settings. This is not generic warning text or a guaranteed vaccine. |
| 18 Unlearning | [Eight Methods to Evaluate Robust Unlearning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.16835) | Remove influence/knowledge/behavior; refusal is not proof of removal. Test extraction, retention, and collateral effects. |
| 19 Data poisoning/backdoors | [Sleeper Agents](https://www.anthropic.com/news/sleeper-agents-training-deceptive-llms-that-persist-through-safety-training) | Poisoning is broader than backdoors; a backdoor is trigger-conditional behavior and can arise through more than data poisoning. Toy shape-classifier example only. |
