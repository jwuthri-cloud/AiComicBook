# AI safety card production

Create one PNG per topic in topics.json, organized into category subfolders. Keep topic order; filename NN-topic-slug.png within its category. Preserve the user's combined topics as single cards, explaining each part. Use the built-in image_gen tool only. Read /Users/julien/.codex/skills/.system/imagegen/SKILL.md and repository README.md. Inspect at least one existing reference with view_image.

## Shared design

Portrait 2:3 educational comic card, consistent series. Near-black #05090B background, ivory #F2EEDF text, cyan #16D9EF dominant, violet #AD70DC and lime #A9D52B panels, amber #F5BE32 takeaway/warning. Bold uppercase slanted brush title, clear large body lettering. Small friendly silver robot with cyan eyes upper left, curious anime/comic human learner upper right; inked contours and cel shading. Subtle circuitry. Clear gutters and restrained glow. Vary from 4 to 10 numbered panels per card, as explicitly requested by the user. Simple topics can use 4–5; broader topics and comparisons should usually use 6–10. Use enough words to preserve the full idea while remaining readable; about 150–280 words is a useful range. Never force every topic into four panels. Render the real technical term in the title, plus a simple subtitle. Flexible teaching sequence: 1) simple idea/analogy, 2) actual mechanism, 3) concrete visual example, 4) why it matters + an important limitation. Adapt layout when paired concepts need comparison. Never let analogy replace the technical meaning. For every technical acronym used on the image, spell out its full words in a prominent subtitle, panel heading, or dedicated STANDS FOR callout (for example, DPO = Direct Preference Optimization). Keep the acronym alongside its expansion so a beginner never has to guess. Review already generated cards and repair missing expansions. The collection-wide AI Safety label does not need repeated expansion. Avoid anthropomorphism implying every AI has desires; distinguish hypothetical mechanisms and experimental evidence from established behavior. Footer one accurate memorable sentence.

## Accuracy and artifacts

For policy use current official Anthropic sources; do not invent ASL thresholds or present draft policy as current. Explain attacks conceptually with harmless toy examples, no usable harmful payloads. Save one exact final prompt per topic in prompts/CATEGORY-NN-slug.txt and a concise source/content note in notes/CATEGORY.md. Generate each separate card with one image_gen call.
