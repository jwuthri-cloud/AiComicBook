# Shared field guide

These three pages teach experimental reasoning and help compare the supplied projects. They are study material, not an official Anthropic publication or employment promise.

## Sources

- User-supplied Fellowship Project Map, updated11Oct2026, preserved in ../source/fellowship-project-map-2026-10-11.txt: project identities, per-project budget ranges, planning assumptions and caveats.
- https://alignment.anthropic.com/2025/anthropic-fellows-program-2026/ — official program describes four months of empirical research, roughly$15k/month compute funding, and mentors pitching ideas that fellows choose and shape. Checked12Oct2026.

The project selection comparisons are original pedagogical synthesis. The source lists nine candidates plus one completed foundation. Its opening says ten ideas, which includes the completed entry. Novelty labels were not exhaustively reverified. Researcher fit is not confirmed mentorship. Per-project estimates are from the supplied map, not quotes. No employment/pay statistics are reproduced.

Experimental advice distinguishes development/test sets, seeds, false positives/negatives, capability and behavior changes, and limits of null results. All toy examples and diagrams are conceptual, not empirical findings.
