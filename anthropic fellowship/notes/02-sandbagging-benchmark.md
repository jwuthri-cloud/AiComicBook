Source: [supplied project map](../source/fellowship-project-map-2026-10-11.txt), project 2. This is a proposed benchmark, not a completed result.

Primary grounding: [Anthropic sabotage evaluations](https://www.anthropic.com/research/sabotage-evaluations) defines controlled sandbagging evaluations and distinguishes them from ordinary low capability; [Auditing hidden objectives](https://www.anthropic.com/research/auditing-hidden-objectives) gives a blind-audit model-organism design; [Automated researchers can subtly sandbag](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2025/automated-researchers-sandbag/) studies monitoring limits in its tested setting; [Natural Language Autoencoders](https://www.anthropic.com/research/natural-language-autoencoders) describes one possible white-box auditing signal.

The core control is an honestly weak group matched on observed task accuracy. Pages require held-out tasks, lock types, false positives, auditor access/cost, confound checks, and reproducibility. The circuit-breaker lock is conditional on feasibility. The supplied map's novelty, model sizes/tool access, and $15–20k compute are unverified planning assumptions; recheck before proposing.
