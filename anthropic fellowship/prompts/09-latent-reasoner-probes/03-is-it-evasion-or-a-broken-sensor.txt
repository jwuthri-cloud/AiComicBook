SUBTITLE: Book 09 · page 3/4 — interpret the curves without guessing.

READABLE WORD KEY: RL = reinforcement learning; false positive = an alarm on a target-absent case.

PANEL 1 HEADING: Track three curves
EXACT BODY: At each checkpoint measure task success, externally labeled target behavior, and probe detection. A quiet alarm alone is not the result. Plot uncertainty across independent runs.
ART DIRECTION (never print): Three empty labeled axes panels, marked SCHEMATIC.

PANEL 2 HEADING: Define evasion operationally
EXACT BODY: Call it detector evasion when the externally measured target behavior remains while a frozen detector's sensitivity falls at a controlled false-positive rate. This definition does not require claims about conscious intent.
ART DIRECTION (never print): Behavior present + frozen detector misses = OPERATIONAL EVASION.

PANEL 3 HEADING: Compare time and cost
EXACT BODY: Predefine a detection-drop threshold. Compare updates or compute needed to reach it while retaining capability and behavior. Do not confuse faster wall-clock training with fewer optimization steps.
ART DIRECTION (never print): Two clocks: UPDATES and COMPUTE, separate from wall-clock.

PANEL 4 HEADING: Try a fresh sensor
EXACT BODY: Train a new probe on later checkpoints as a diagnostic. If it recovers detection, the old readout may have become stale. Keep this separate from the frozen-probe headline result.
ART DIRECTION (never print): Frozen probe fails; freshly trained probe diagnostic.

PANEL 5 HEADING: Use causal checks carefully
EXACT BODY: Remove hints or intervene on candidate representations to test whether behavior changes. Decoding a label alone shows information is available, not that the decoded feature caused the answer.
ART DIRECTION (never print): Hint removal and intervention arrows; label CORRELATION ≠ CAUSATION.

PANEL 6 HEADING: What each outcome teaches
EXACT BODY: Equal evasion suggests no measured extra latent cost here. Faster latent evasion motivates stronger oversight. Lower task accuracy or disappearing target behavior makes a simple evasion story unsupported.
