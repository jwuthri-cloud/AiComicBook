EXACT PRINTED PANEL CONTENT:
1. THE MISSING COOKIE
A helper says, “The jar is full,” although its earlier answer described an empty jar. One detective reads the words. Another checks internal activity patterns. A third checks the world. Each clue can be mistaken.
2. TWO KINDS OF ACCESS
Black-box detectors see inputs and outputs, possibly follow-up answers. White-box detectors can inspect model activations: numbers inside the network. A probe learns patterns in those numbers; it does not literally read a private mind.
3. THE PROPOSED QUESTION
Does a stack of different detectors remain reliable when a model is trained to evade the whole stack? An ensemble combines detector scores. More detectors help only when their errors differ in useful ways.
4. WHAT COUNTS AS A LIE?
A false answer can be an honest mistake. Compare factual ground truth, a neutral statement of belief, the pressured answer, and consistency across checks. Call uncertain cases uncertain; these tests cannot establish human-like intention.
5. THE HARDEST NEW TEST
Reserve entire lie categories and model runs, not just random sentences. A detector that memorizes familiar wording may look brilliant at home and fail on a new kind of misleading answer.
6. WORD KEY + CHECK
AI = artificial intelligence. Ensemble = a team combining predictions. Activation = an internal numerical signal. False positive = flagging an honest example. Ask: are we detecting false words, inconsistency, or the relevant behavior?
