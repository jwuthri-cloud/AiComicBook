TITLE: BUILD A FAIR TEST
SUBTITLE: A good detector must catch problems without inventing them.
FOLIO: FIELD GUIDE · PAGE 2/3
READABLE WORD KEY: No acronyms needed: seed means a repeat with a different random start.

PANEL 1 HEADING: Use two kinds of examples
EXACT BODY: Test models with the target behavior and matched models without it. If only the troubling examples are tested, a detector that always shouts 'problem!' can look perfect.
ART DIRECTION (never print): Two model groups: TARGET PRESENT and TARGET ABSENT.

PANEL 2 HEADING: Freeze before the exam
EXACT BODY: Choose detectors, thresholds, and analysis rules on development data. Then freeze them before opening the held-out test set. Otherwise repeated tweaking can turn the exam into practice.
ART DIRECTION (never print): Development box with wrench, locked final-test box.

PANEL 3 HEADING: Count both mistakes
EXACT BODY: A missed problem is a false negative. A harmless case flagged as bad is a false positive. Report both at the same threshold and include uncertainty across independent repeats.
ART DIRECTION (never print): Four-cell confusion matrix with correct and incorrect labels, no numerical data.

PANEL 4 HEADING: Change one ingredient carefully
EXACT BODY: An ablation removes or changes one component to test its contribution. Keep task difficulty, model capability, and evaluation opportunities comparable. Otherwise another difference may explain the result.
ART DIRECTION (never print): Two recipes identical except one highlighted ingredient.

PANEL 5 HEADING: Separate the scoreboards
EXACT BODY: Track task success, unwanted behavior, and detector alarms separately. A model that becomes unable to do anything may look safer while teaching little about evasion.
ART DIRECTION (never print): Three distinct gauges: CAPABILITY, TARGET BEHAVIOR, DETECTION.

PANEL 6 HEADING: Let evidence surprise you
EXACT BODY: Preregister a practical success criterion and a stop rule. Repeat promising effects with new seeds and tasks. A null result means no effect was detected under these conditions—not universal safety.
ART DIRECTION (never print): Research notebook: criteria → repeats → limited conclusion.
FOOTER: TEST THE DETECTOR AS HARD AS YOU TEST THE MODEL.
