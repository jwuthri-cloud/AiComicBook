PANELS:
1. THE QUESTION
A gardener checks a plant as it grows, not only at the end. This proposal checks model checkpoints across training for early warning signs.
2. WHAT IS A CHECKPOINT?
A checkpoint is a saved version of the model at one training step. A sequence can show when a measured behavior appeared.
3. WHICH SIGNS?
Track spoken test awareness, activation-probe signals, reward seeking in controlled tasks, and persona-vector movement.
4. COMPARE WITH ACTIONS
Run behavioral evals at the same checkpoints. Ask whether an internal signal changes before held-out behavior does.
5. USE KNOWN CASES
Include deliberately constructed positive controls with a known target behavior, plus ordinary runs as negatives.
6. WHAT IT CANNOT CLAIM
A pattern in activations is a clue, not proof of secret plans. Earlier detection in a few runs does not guarantee broad prediction.
